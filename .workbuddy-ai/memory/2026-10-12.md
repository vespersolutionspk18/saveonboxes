Key design points noted:
- Claim is transactional (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE on label), idempotent via eventId, rate-limited per IP+user.
- Labels: 128-bit random token stored as hash + encrypted ciphertext; serial is human-readable; one active box per label.
- Box naming: falls back to label short_serial, then "Box N".
- Search spans box name/notes, box_number, item name/notes, label serials.
- Frontend is careful: mutation IDs to prevent duplicate item adds, photo resize/compress client-side to <=3.5MB, abortable fetch with timeouts.

## Category subpages: 15 Uline-scraped category pages

Built `/category/<slug>` for every one of the 15 homepage cards, modelled on
Uline group pages (reference: `Grp_141/Moving-Boxes`). Product detail pages
deliberately NOT built — user said "dont build the individual product pages yet".

- `scripts/fetch-uline-category.mjs` — the scraper.
  `node scripts/fetch-uline-category.mjs <slug> <uline-url> <group|products>`
  - `group` mode: regex-scrapes a `Grp_*` page (featured row + `clsgrpthumbnails`).
  - `products` mode: headless Chrome `--dump-dom` on a `BL_*` page, pulls SKUs out
    of `<table class="subgroupChart">`, names from the `dataProductInformation`
    data island, images derived from the SKU
    (`https://img.uline.com/is/image/uline/<SKU>` — Uline's image server resolves
    any item number, so images never need to be found in markup).
  - `MIN_PRODUCTS = 4` heuristic picks the first product table when it's big
    enough, otherwise unions all product tables. Caps at 28 cards.
- `scripts/fetch-all-categories.mjs` — `SOURCES` array of all 15
  `[slug, url, mode]`; pass slugs to re-run a subset.
- `src/app/category-data/*.json` (15) + `index.js` registry — `index.js` overrides
  each page `title` with our own `movingCategories` label and computes `to`
  cross-links between sibling categories via a normalised-label map.
- `src/app/components/CategoryPage.js` — breadcrumb, uppercase H1, featured row
  of 3 (black label bars), divider, then the 4-per-row grid.
- `src/app/category/[slug]/page.js` — `generateStaticParams` / `generateMetadata`
  / `notFound()`. `params` is a Promise in Next 16 (`await params`).
- `public/categories/<slug>/*.webp` — 214 product images across 15 folders.

Uline taxonomy learned: `Cls_*` → `Grp_*` → `BL_*` → `Product/Detail/S-*`.
`Grp_*` pages render server-side; `BL_*` pages render their product table
client-side (need headless Chrome). `Product/AdvSearchResult?keywords=x*` URLs
are JavaScript-only and unscrapable — three hub cards (Artwork/Mirror, Golf,
Bike) use them, so they were mapped to the closest real pages: `BL_7901`,
`BL_410/Tall-Boxes`, `BL_413/Large-Side-Loaders`. No dedicated golf-box or
bike-box page exists on Uline (verified against the full sitemap).

Re-running a category leaves orphaned webp files — `rm -rf public/categories/<slug>`
before re-fetching.

Verified: all 15 `/category/<slug>` return 200, unknown slug 404s, homepage 200,
and `npm run build` prerenders all 15 as SSG. Not committed yet.

## Cross-referenced Uline's products against our supplier catalogue

Two scripts added:
- `scripts/cross-reference.mjs` — matches Uline's scraped entries against our
  531 supplier products (New Haven 337 + CDS 194) and writes
  `reports/product-cross-reference.json`.
- `scripts/build-comparison-report.mjs` — renders
  `reports/product-comparison.html` plus three CSVs
  (`1-both-carry.csv`, `2-ours-only.csv`, `3-uline-only.csv`).

Matching model: classify both sides into a canonical product *type*, then compare
on type; dimensions confirm a match within a type. "Exact size" > "same product"
> "adjacent". Types where we carry something in the same family but not the same
product are flagged in a `LOOSE_TYPES` map so the report doesn't overstate.

Results: Uline's 205 scraped rows collapse to 195 unique entries (108 product
SKUs + 87 category links). 108 match something we carry, 87 are Uline-only,
211 of our products have no Uline counterpart.

Key findings:
- We carry zero tall boxes (4x4xN / 5x5xN), zero side-loading boxes, zero guitar
  boxes, zero racquet boxes — these are the bulk of Uline's golf/bike/artwork
  range.
- Only 4 exact dimension matches in the whole comparison.
- We carry Pak-N-Move *components* (bags, cell kits, knife, newspaper) but not
  bundled moving kits.
- Our catalogue is weighted to moving equipment and cargo control (straps 56,
  safety 25, ramps 20, panel carts 16) which Uline's 15 moving-box categories
  don't cover at all.

Classification gotchas worth remembering:
- Rule order in the `TYPES` array is load-bearing. `generic-box` must stay LAST
  or it swallows everything containing "box"/"carton".
- Type our products from the **product name first**, falling back to category.
  Category labels are noisy — "Speed pack/commercial bins" was turning a Gaylord
  pallet box into a speed pack.
- Check equipment (dolly/hand truck) before loose supply words, or
  "dolly with foam wheels" becomes packing foam.
- Never let the `unclassified` bucket match itself.

## Does our catalogue fit the 15 Uline-derived categories?

Answer: **no**. 223 of 531 fit cleanly, 113 fit loosely, **195 (36.7%) have no
home at all**.

- Extracted the taxonomy out of `cross-reference.mjs` into
  `scripts/lib/taxonomy.mjs` (shared by both analysis scripts). Refactor was
  verified identical against the previous output before moving on.
- New `scripts/category-coverage.mjs` → `reports/category-coverage.json`,
  `reports/4-category-coverage.csv`, `reports/category-coverage.html`.
  It maps each product type to the best of the 15 categories with a
  `fits` / `loose` / `none` rating, and suggests a new category for the rest.

Findings:
- 3 of the 15 categories get **zero** of our products: Moving Kits, Golf, Bike.
- "Moving Blankets, Pads and Supplies" is doing too much work — 131 products,
  84 of them a stretch (57 dollies, 15 hand trucks, 17 pads, 10 covers). Uline
  files one hardwood dolly there; we carry dollies as a whole product line.
- "Packing Supplies" is the cleanest fit: 96 products, all clean.
- 9 suggested new categories cover the 195 homeless products, the big ones being
  Straps & Tie-Downs (67), Cargo Control (31), Safety & PPE (25),
  Ramps & Walkboards (20), Carts & Platform Trucks (18).

Taxonomy gaps closed along the way (these were silently mis-bucketing real
products): plural `PADS` was not matching `\bpad\b`; `CUSHION FOAM` and
`STYROFOAM SHEET` weren't foam; corrugated sheets, neoprene floor runners,
logistic track/sleeve and pallet jacks were all unclassified. Unclassified went
from 57 to 7.

**Bug fixed in `cross-reference.mjs`:** `ulineTypes` included the literal
`"unclassified"` string, so our unclassified products were excluded from
`oursOnly` *and* never matched — they dropped out of the report entirely. Now
filtered out. `oursOnly` went 211 → 263. Our side reconciles: 268 matched-type +
263 ours-only = 531.
