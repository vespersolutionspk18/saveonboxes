# 2026-10-13 — Replace the inventory with Uline's

## What was asked

"Replace our whole inventory with the uline inventory, the one we used to make
the categories and sub categories. get all the information from their products
and variants."

So: `src/app/supplier-catalog.json` stops being New Haven + CDS and becomes
Uline's real product catalogue, with full detail per SKU.

## The network problem, and how it was solved

Node's `fetch` cannot reach `www.uline.com` at all (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`),
and neither can `WebFetch`. `curl` works only through the local proxy already
exported as `HTTPS_PROXY`, and that proxy is intermittent — roughly half of
requests fail, with 502s on CONNECT. Everything now goes through
`scripts/lib/uline-fetch.mjs`, which shells out to curl and retries with
backoff. Headless Chrome was tried and is not needed: the browse pages are
server-side rendered.

## Pipeline (all in `scripts/`)

1. `uline-enumerate.mjs` — walks the 15 category pages, expands `Grp_*` group
   hubs to `BL_*` browse pages, and collects every SKU. 231 browse pages →
   **446 unique SKUs**. Writes `reports/uline-skus.json`. Supports re-running a
   subset without clobbering the rest.
2. `uline-fetch-products.mjs --shard N --of M` — fetches each SKU's product
   page. Resumable: rewrites its output after every product. Writes
   `reports/uline-products/shard-N-of-M.json`.
3. `uline-fetch-images.mjs --shard N --of M` — pulls
   `img.uline.com/is/image/uline/<SKU>`, re-encodes to WebP with sharp into
   `public/uline/<SKU>.webp`. Skips files already present.
4. `build-uline-catalog.mjs` — merges the shards, dedupes by SKU, assigns each
   product a primary category, and writes `src/app/supplier-catalog.json`.
   Backs up the previous catalogue to `reports/supplier-catalog.pre-uline.json`.

Result: **446 products, 0 failures, all 15 categories populated.** 434 carry a
price; 12 do not (see below).

## Uline page anatomy (the useful part)

Two data islands per product page:

- **schema.org JSON-LD** in `<script type="application/ld&#x2B;json">` — name,
  description, sku, image, colour, the `size` triple, width/height, and `offers`
  carrying both the per-unit price and the bundle price + bundle quantity.
  Parse the JSON **before** decoding entities: Uline escapes embedded quotes as
  `&quot;`, which is what keeps it valid.
- **`dataLayer.push({ 'event': 'Product_Details', ... })`** — Uline's own
  taxonomy: `productClassDesc`, `productGroupDesc`, `productSubgroupDesc`,
  `productItemDesc`, `productPrice`.

A `BL_*` page is a *family*; the SKUs on it are the *variants*. For the
catalogue we group families by Uline's **subgroup**, not by browse page —
browse-page membership depends on crawl order, the subgroup does not.

## Bugs found and fixed

1. **Entity decoding needed to iterate, and strip tags inside the loop.**
   Uline double-escapes: `&reg;` arrives as `&amp;reg;`, and inline markup is
   escaped too — `Keepbox<sup>&trade;</sup>` arrives as
   `Keepbox&lt;sup&gt;&amp;trade;&lt;/sup&gt;`. A single pass leaves `&reg;` and
   `<sup>`. `decodeEntities` now loops (max 4 passes) doing strip-then-decode
   each time. 22 records were affected, one of which (`S-19938`) needed the
   tag-stripping to be inside the loop.
2. **12 product pages carry no JSON-LD at all** — Uline's Round Trip Totes
   template (plus a pallet-marker page). No schema.org block, no
   `Product_Details` island, and the price is never rendered into the DOM even
   after the page's own JavaScript runs. `parseProductPageFallback` scrapes the
   `hdnItemDescription` field and the `ProductDetailHierarchyData` ids instead,
   and marks the record `partial: true` with a null price. The storefront
   already renders a null price as "Price coming soon".

## Storefront wiring

`src/app/supplier-products.js` was rewritten to consume the new catalogue. It
maps each product's primary category onto the type/categoryId vocabulary the
existing pages use, and adds seven new `supplierBoxCategories` sections (golf,
bike, lamp, bulk-cargo, round-trip-totes on top of mattress and moving-kits) so
`/boxes` shows a section per category. `catalog.js` needed no change — it picks
those up through `supplierBoxCategories`.

## Categorisation + product pages

Asked for: "categorize them, based on the uline categories we made on the
homepage and their subcategories. and build their individual pages, with all the
info we pulled from uline. and it has to look like an actual product page, not
what uline has for product pages. keep things compact and data driven and
simple."

### `src/app/catalog-tree.js` (new) — the single source of truth

Everything the category and product pages render comes from here:

- `allProducts` — `[...supplierBoxProducts, ...supplierSupplyProducts]` = 446
- `subcategoryName(product)` — the Uline **subgroup** (`ulineTaxonomy.subgroup`),
  trademark symbols stripped. 12 products have no taxonomy at all; for those it
  takes the family from the product name (`"<family> - <variant>"` split on
  `" - "`). That is what turns `S-12700 "Uline Pallet Markers - Chisel-Tip"` into
  the subcategory "Uline Pallet Markers" instead of a junk group named after the
  category.
- `categoryTree` / `getCategoryTree(slug)` — 15 categories, each
  `{id, label, image, href, count, subcategories, products}`; subcategories
  `{id, name, products}` sorted by count desc then name.
- `getProductBySlug` / `productsBySlug`, `relatedProducts`, `imageFor`,
  `formatPrice`, `productSpecs`.

Result: **446 products, 15 categories, 74 subcategories, no empty category, no
subcategory slug collisions, no duplicate SKUs.**

### Routes

- `/category/[slug]` — rewritten to read `categoryTree` instead of the stale
  `src/app/category-data/*.json` (205 cards, 88 of which we do not stock).
  Server component, no client JS: breadcrumb, title, count, subcategory jump
  chips (only when ≥2), then one `<section id="sub-…">` grid per subcategory.
  Every card links to `/product/<slug>`.
- `/product/[slug]` — **new**, 446 SSG pages. Breadcrumb, image + facts column,
  `productSpecs()` table, description, 8 siblings. No tabs/carousels/reviews.
  `ProductBuyPanel` is the only client component (quantity stepper +
  add-to-cart via `useStore`).

### Gotchas hit

- `formatPrice` and `productImage` live in `Storefront.js`, which is
  `"use client"` — you **cannot** call them during a server render. Hence the
  duplicates in `catalog-tree.js`.
- `uline.size` is not always 3-part: labels and folders are 2-part (`5x3`,
  `14.75x9.625`). `formatSize` in `supplier-products.js` now handles 2 or 3.
- `additional_categories` is a byte-for-byte duplicate of `uline.subgroup` in
  all 446 records. Use `ulineTaxonomy.subgroup`; ignore the other.
- 434/446 have `details` (description), `variants` (a human string like
  `15 × 12 × 10" · White · 25 per bundle`), `availability` ("In stock"),
  `condition` ("New") and a price. The 12 without are the `partial: true` ones.
- `npm run start` binds `0.0.0.0`; use `npx next start --hostname 127.0.0.1`.
- Background servers started with `nohup … &` inside a Bash call are killed when
  that call returns. Use the Bash tool's `run_in_background` instead.
